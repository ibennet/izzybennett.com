---
title: "Raspberry Lemon Scones"
category: "breakfast"
tags: ["scones", "raspberry", "lemon", "baking"]
tools:
  - "1 large mixing bowl"
  - "1 bowl"
  - "Spatula"
  - "Baking sheet"
  - "Grater"
ingredients:
  - group: "Scones"
    items:
      - "3 cups flour"
      - "⅓ cup sugar"
      - "1 tbsp baking powder"
      - "1 tsp salt"
      - "10 tbsp butter"
      - "½ tsp vanilla extract"
      - "2 tbsp lemon zest"
      - "¾ cup milk"
      - "3-6 oz raspberries"
  - group: "Lemon Icing"
    items:
      - "1 cup powdered sugar"
      - "1-2 tbsp lemon juice"
      - "½ tsp vanilla extract"
steps:
  - "Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large mixing bowl then set aside."
  - "Cut butter into cubes. Add butter to dry mixture."
  - "Mix butter and dry ingredients with hands or two forks until the butter pieces are no larger than peas."
  - "Mix in the vanilla extract, lemon zest, and milk with a spatula."
  - "Add in the raspberries."
  - "On a floured surface, shape the dough into a large circle. Then cut the dough like a pizza into 8 slices."
  - "Chill the dough in the fridge for at least 30 minutes."
  - "Remove dough from fridge and brush the scones with milk."
  - "Bake at 375ºF for 20-30 minutes or until the tops are golden."
  - "In a small bowl, mix together the powdered sugar, lemon juice, and vanilla extract."
  - "Drizzle icing onto the scones."
---
