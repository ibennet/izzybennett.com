---
title: "Pizza Dough"
category: "other"
keywords: ["bread", "dough", "italian"]
tools:
  - "Large mixing bowl"
  - "Small bowl"
  - "Damp towel"
ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 cups 00 flour"
      - "1 tsp sugar"
      - "1 tbsp salt"
      - "1 cup warm water"
      - "1 package active dry yeast"
      - "1 tsp extra virgin olive oil"
steps:
  - items:
      - "In a large mixing bowl, mix together the flour and salt."
      - "In a small bowl, mix together the water, yeast, and sugar."
      - "Let yeast sit for about 5 to 10 minutes."
      - "Pour in the yeast mixture to the flour mixture."
      - "Knead dough for 5 minutes or until it easily comes off the sides of the bowl."
      - "Oil the dough ball and then put back in bowl and cover with plastic wrap."
      - "Let dough rise for 1 hour."
      - "Roll dough into cylinder and cut into 3 equal pieces. Let dough rest for 10 minutes."
      - "When baking this dough, bake at 450°F for 12-15 minutes."
---
