---
title: "Apple Bread"
category: "dessert"
keywords: ["bread", "apple", "baking"]
tools:
  - "1 large mixing bowl"
  - "2 small mixing bowls"
  - "Bread pan"
  - "Egg beater"
  - "Cutting board"
  - "Peeler"
ingredients:
  - group: "Bread layer"
    items:
      - "8 tbsp softened butter"
      - "⅔ cup sugar"
      - "2 eggs"
      - "1 ½ tsp vanilla extract"
      - "1 ½ cup flour"
      - "1 ½ tsp baking powder"
      - "½ cup oat milk"
  - group: "Apple layer"
    items:
      - "½ cup brown sugar"
      - "2 tsp ground cinnamon"
      - "2 medium apples"
  - group: "Icing"
    items:
      - "2 tbsp oat milk"
      - "½ tsp vanilla extract"
      - "1 cup powdered sugar"
steps:
  - items:
      - "Preheat oven to 350º F."
      - "Place butter and sugar in large mixing bowl. Beat with egg beater until combined."
      - "Add in eggs and vanilla extract. Beat until combined."
      - "Add in flour and baking powder. Combine."
      - "Add in oat milk."
      - "In smaller mixing bowl, combine brown sugar and ground cinnamon."
      - "Peel the apples and then finely chop them."
      - "In the bread pan, spread half of the bread layer. Then place half the chopped apples on top and slightly press them into the batter."
      - "Coat the apples with half of the brown sugar and cinnamon mix."
      - "Repeat with remaining bread layer and apple layer."
      - "Bake for 50 minutes."
      - "Let bread completely cool, then mix the icing ingredients in a small bowl. Coat bread with icing."
---
