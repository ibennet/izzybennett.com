---
title: "Apple Pie"
category: "dessert"
keywords: ["pie", "apple", "baking"]
tools:
  - "2 large mixing bowls"
  - "Plastic wrap"
  - "Rolling pin"
  - "Pie dish"
ingredients:
  - group: "Pie Crust"
    items:
      - "2 ¼ cup flour"
      - "1 tbsp sugar"
      - "½ tsp salt"
      - "8 tbsp plant butter"
      - "½ cup shortening"
      - "7 tbsp cold water"
      - "2 tbsp plant milk"
  - group: "Apple Filling"
    items:
      - "6-8 apples"
      - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
      - "¼ cup flour"
      - "⅓ cup sugar"
      - "¼ cup brown sugar"
      - "1 tsp cinnamon"
      - "¼ tsp nutmeg"
      - "¼ tsp salt"
      - "1 lemon"
steps:
  - items:
      - "Peel and slice the apples. While filling bowl with sliced apples, continue to squeeze the lemon over the slices to prevent browning."
      - "Add the vanilla, flour, sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt to the bowl of apples and mix together with hands until apples are coated evenly."
      - "Cut the plant butter into small cubes."
      - "Add the flour, sugar, and salt to a mixing bowl. Stir. Then add the plant butter cubes, and then the shortening 1 tbsp at a time. Add the water. Squeeze and knead the ingredients together to form a dough."
      - "Cut the dough into two evenly sized balls and wrap one in plastic wrap and put in fridge."
      - "Flour a surface and roll out the other dough ball so that it is the size of the pie dish."
      - "Place the rolled out dough into the pie dish and fill with apple slices. Take out the other dough from the fridge, roll it out, and place on top."
      - "Brush pie crust with milk."
      - "Place assembled pie in fridge for 30 minutes."
      - "Bake pie at 400º F for 30 minutes. Then lower temperature to 350º F and bake an additional 50 minutes."
      - "Let pie cool for 2 hours."
---
