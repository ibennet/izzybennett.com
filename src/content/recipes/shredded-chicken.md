---
title: "Shredded Chicken"
category: "main"
keywords: ["chicken", "instant-pot", "meal-prep"]
tools:
  - "Meat tenderizer"
  - "Instant Pot"
  - "Instant read meat thermometer"
  - "Tongs"
ingredients:
  - items:
      - "2 chicken breasts"
      - "1 cup chicken broth"
      - "Seasonings if desired"
steps:
  - "Tenderize the chicken breasts so that they are evenly thick."
  - "If using other seasonings, mix seasonings in bowl and then coat chicken breasts with seasoning. Let marinate in fridge for at least 1 hour."
  - "Add the chicken broth and chicken breasts to the Instant Pot."
  - "Set to high pressure and 10 minutes, then press start."
  - "Let natural release for 5 minutes, or 10 minutes if the chicken breasts were frozen. Ensure the chicken is 165°F."
---
