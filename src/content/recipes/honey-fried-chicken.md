steps:
  - items:
      - "Cut the chicken into 1-inch cubes."
      - "Put flour in one bowl and buttermilk in the other."
      - "Coat the chicken cubes in flour. Then coat them with the buttermilk. Then coat them again with flour."
      - "Heat vegetable oil in frying pan over medium heat."
      - "Place chicken cubes in frying pan and cook on each side for 3-4 minutes or until crispy. Then transfer cooked chicken on to a paper towel lined plate."
      - "In a separate bowl combine the cornstarch and water."
      - "In a saucepan combine the honey, apple cider vinegar, sesame oil, and soy sauce. Bring to a boil, and then reduce heat to low and add cornstarch mixture. Continue to occasionally stir until sauce is thick, then turn off heat and set aside."
      - "Combine the sauce and chicken."
notes:
  - "To coat the chicken quickly, place half of the cubed chicken in the bowl of flour and mix until coated evenly. Then place the coated chicken into the buttermilk and coat evenly. Once again place chicken into flour and coat evenly."
  - "If sauce is not thickening, try adding another cornstarch mixture or increasing the heat."
