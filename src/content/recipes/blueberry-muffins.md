---
title: "Blueberry Muffins"
category: "dessert"
keywords: ["muffins", "blueberry", "baking"]
tools:
  - "1 large mixing bowl"
  - "2 bowls"
  - "Muffin pan for 12 muffins"
  - "Grater"
ingredients:
  - group: "Muffin"
    items:
      - "1 cup milk with 1 tbsp vinegar OR 1 cup yogurt"
      - "½ cup and 2 tbsp sugar"
      - "6 tbsp canola oil"
      - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
      - "1 lemon"
      - "2 cups flour"
      - "2 ½ tsp baking powder"
      - "¼ tsp baking soda"
      - "½ tsp salt"
      - "1 pint blueberries"
  - group: "Streusel Topping"
    items:
      - "¼ cup brown sugar"
      - "½ tsp ground cinnamon"
      - "2 tsp vegetable oil"
      - "1 tbsp flour"
steps:
  - "Combine milk and vinegar then set aside."
  - "Take the lemon and grate it to get lemon zest."
  - "In a large mixing bowl combine sugar, canola oil, vanilla extract, and lemon zest. Then mix in the milk and vinegar mixture."
  - "In another bowl, combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt."
  - "Slowly mix in the dry ingredients with the wet ingredients. Once thoroughly mixed, add in blueberries."
  - "Pour the muffin mix into a muffin pan."
  - "In a small bowl, combine the brown sugar, ground cinnamon, vegetable oil, and flour."
  - "Put the streusel mixture on each muffin."
  - "Bake at 375º F for 25-30 minutes or until the tops start to turn golden brown."
  - "Let cool for 10 minutes."
---
