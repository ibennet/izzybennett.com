---
title: "Sweet and Spicy Sauce"
category: "sauce"
keywords: ["sauce", "gochujang", "condiment"]
tools:
  - "Sauce pan"
ingredients:
  - items:
      - "1 ½ tbsp ketchup"
      - "1 tbsp gochujang"
      - "2 tbsp honey"
      - "2 tbsp brown sugar"
      - "1 tbsp soy sauce"
      - "1 tsp garlic powder"
      - "½ tsp sesame oil"
steps:
  - "Combine all ingredients into sauce pan."
  - "Bring to a boil while stirring, then reduce to medium heat."
  - "While stirring occasionally, let the sauce thicken for around 5 minutes."
  - "Remove from heat."
---
