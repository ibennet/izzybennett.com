---
title: "Cherry Tomato Pasta"
category: "main"
keywords: ["pasta", "tomato"]
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 20
servings: 8
ingredients:
  - items:
      - "4 shallots"
      - "4 cloves garlic"
      - "8 tbsp butter"
      - "24oz cherry or sungold tomatoes"
      - "1lb spaghetti"
      - "1 tbsp sugar"
      - "1oz basil"
      - "8oz burrata"
      - "2 tbsp Olive oil"
steps:
  - "Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook the **spaghetti** per its instructions."
  - "Finely chop the **shallots**."
  - "Mince the **garlic**."
  - "Crush the **tomatoes**. I use two baking sheets, place the tomatoes on one, and then lay the other baking sheet on top of the tomatoes, and then press down to smush all the tomatoes."
  - "Heat the **butter** and **olive oil** in a large pan."
  - "Once the butter has melted, add the **shallots** and **garlic**. Cook for **1 minute**."
  - "Add the **tomatoes** to the pan. Cook until the tomatoes become soft. About **5 minutes**."
  - "Add **salt** to the pan to season. Start with 2 tsp and add more if needed."
  - "Add the **sugar** to the pan. Stir."
  - "Turn the heat off the pan and add the cooked spaghetti to the pan. Mix everything."
  - "Transfer the spaghetti to a large bowl and top with the **basil** and **burrata**."
---
