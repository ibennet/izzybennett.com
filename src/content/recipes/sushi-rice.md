---
title: "Sushi Rice"
category: "side"
keywords: ["rice", "japanese"]
tools:
  - "Pot"
  - "Sauce pan"
ingredients:
  - items:
      - "3 cups water"
      - "2 cups uncooked sushi rice"
      - "1 tbsp vegetable oil"
      - "¼ cup sugar"
      - "¼ cup sushi vinegar"
      - "1 tsp salt"
steps:
  - items:
      - "Bring water to a boil in a pot. Then add in rice, reduce heat to low, and cook until all the water is absorbed."
      - "In the sauce pan, combine the vegetable oil, sugar, sushi vinegar, and salt. Cook with medium heat until the sugar has melted. Set aside."
      - "Once the rice has finished cooking, add in the sugar mixture and mix."
      - "Let cool."
---
